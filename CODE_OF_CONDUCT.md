# Code of Conduct · 行为准则

**English** · [简体中文](#简体中文)

## Our pledge

We want participation in Yonder Harness to be a harassment-free, respectful experience for everyone, regardless of age, background, identity, experience level, nationality or beliefs.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive; critique code and ideas, not people.
- Assume good faith, and explain your reasoning.
- Keep discussion on topic; share only information you are allowed to share, and never post other people's credentials or private data.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, discrimination, threats or personal attacks.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.
- Spam, or deliberately disruptive behavior.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove contributions that violate this code, and may restrict participation. Report concerns to zoranxc.work@gmail.com; reports are handled confidentially. This code applies in project spaces and when representing the project in public.

## 简体中文

### 我们的承诺

希望每个人参与 Yonder Harness 时都不受骚扰、受到尊重，无论年龄、背景、身份、经验、国籍或信仰。

### 期望的行为

- 尊重并建设性地交流：评价代码和想法，而不是人。
- 以善意推定对方，并说明你的理由。
- 讨论保持切题；只分享你有权分享的信息，不要发布他人的凭据或隐私。

### 不可接受的行为

- 骚扰、辱骂、歧视、威胁或人身攻击。
- 未经许可公开他人的私人信息。
- 垃圾信息或故意捣乱。

### 执行

维护者可以编辑、隐藏或删除违反本准则的内容，并限制其参与。请将问题发送到 zoranxc.work@gmail.com，我们会保密处理。本准则适用于项目的各类空间，以及在公开场合代表项目时。
